Design a polished, production-quality single-page marketing website for Bonjou — an open-source terminal (CLI) app for local-network chat and file transfer. PRODUCT Bonjou lets people on the same Wi-Fi or LAN chat and send files and folders directly between their machines — no accounts, no cloud, no central server, no internet required. It auto-discovers nearby peers over UDP broadcast and transfers data over TCP. Transfers are metadata-first: the receiver sees exactly what is being sent and must explicitly approve it before any bytes are written to disk. Bonjou is written in Go, MIT-licensed, cross-platform (macOS, Linux, Windows), and runs entirely in the terminal with a fast @-command interface plus an optional guided wizard. Current version 1.2.0. AUDIENCE Developers, technical teams, students and instructors, hackathon and workshop participants — terminal-comfortable people who need to move messages and files across a room without cloud tools.
